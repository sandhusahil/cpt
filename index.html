PERSONAL OPERATING SYSTEM

CPT
Code of Personal Transformation

A structured framework for building discipline, consistency, fitness, career growth, digital control, spirituality, and long-term personal excellence.

```

Core Pillars

The foundation of a disciplined and meaningful life.

1

Fitness

Morning Routine

  • Wake up at 6:00 AM

Exercise

  • Walk / Run / Workout for at least 30 minutes daily

Healthy Eating

  • Follow a balanced diet
  • Avoid overeating and unnecessary junk food

Mental Fitness

  • Learning, reading, problem-solving, cognitive growth

Rest & Recovery

  • Sleep 7–8 hours daily
  • Sleep between 10:30 PM – 11:00 PM
2

Career Growth

Daily Learning

  • Power BI, SQL, Alteryx, Analytics, and related skills

Project Building

  • Build practical portfolio projects

Job Search

  • Apply for jobs and prepare for interviews

Continuous Improvement

  • Learn skills that improve employability
3

Digital Discipline

Content Quality

  • Positive, educational, meaningful content only
  • No harmful content

YouTube Videos

  • Allowed only after 2 PM

YouTube Shorts

  • Allowed only after 5 PM

Movies & Entertainment

  • Allowed between 10:00 AM – 10:30 PM
  • Work first, entertainment later

Digital Boundaries

  • No phone for first 30 minutes after waking
  • No random browsing
  • Keep unnecessary notifications off
4

Spiritual Well-Being

Meditation & Prayer

  • Practice daily meditation and/or prayer

Introspection

  • Reflect on thoughts, actions and behavior

Spiritual Study

  • Read or listen to spiritual teachings

Gratitude

  • Practice daily gratitude
5

Revision & Adaptation

Revise Only When Needed

  • Avoid unnecessary changes

Core Pillars Stay Unchanged

  • Fitness
  • Career Growth
  • Digital Discipline
  • Spiritual Well-Being
  • Accountability

Reflect on Alignment

  • Ensure actions align with long-term goals

Structured Updates

  • Improve thoughtfully, not emotionally
6

Accountability

Daily Questions

  • Did I exercise?
  • Did I work on my career?
  • Did I maintain digital discipline?
  • Did I meditate/pray?

Purpose

  • Build discipline, honesty and consistency

Scoring System

4 / 4 Excellent Day
3 / 4 Good Day
2 / 4 Average Day
0–1 / 4 Recovery Needed

Streak Rules

  • Streak continues with Excellent or Good days.
  • Streak breaks with Average or Recovery days.
  • Never lie while scoring.

Recovery Rule

  • Accept it honestly.
  • Do not punish yourself.
  • Restart the next day.
  • Focus on consistency, not perfection.

Integrity Rule

  • Rewards are not the goal.
  • Discipline and honesty are the goal.
  • Rewards are earned only when genuine.

Reward Milestones

3-Day Streak

Favorite snack or drink under ₹100

7-Day Streak

Treat under ₹150

10-Day Streak

One guilt-free movie in a cinema/theater

14-Day Streak

Favorite meal or outing under ₹250

21-Day Streak

Buy something useful under ₹500

30-Day Streak

Buy something meaningful for self-improvement

OR

Take a full leisure evening without guilt

```
